Ben Davis

I'm a fourth year Ph.D. student at UC Berkeley with Hannah Stuart. My research interests involve robot design for manipulation, with a focus on building and learning with hardware that's mechanically robust and adaptable for real-world use. My work is supported by the NSF Graduate Research Fellowship.
Before coming to Berkeley, I did my undergrad at The University of Texas at Austin, where I worked with Nanshu Lu on soft wearable sensing and Ann Majewicz Fey on haptic motor learning.
