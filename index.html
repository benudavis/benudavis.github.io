Ben Davis

I'm a fourth year Ph.D. student at UC Berkeley with Hannah Stuart. My research interests involve robot design for manipulation, with a focus on building and learning with hardware that's mechanically robust and adaptable for real-world use. I'm supported by the NSF Graduate Research Fellowship.

I did my undergrad at The University of Texas at Austin, where I worked on soft wearable sensing with Nanshu Lu and haptic motor learning with Ann Majewicz Fey.
